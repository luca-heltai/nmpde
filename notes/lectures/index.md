# Lectures

This section collects the theoretical material developed during the lectures.
The order follows the course calendar and will be expanded throughout the semester.

[Lecture 3 — Polynomial approximation and a priori error estimates](lecture-03.md)
develops the Deny–Lions and Bramble–Hilbert lemmas and their application to
conforming Galerkin error estimates.

[Lecture 4 — Finite elements, dual bases, and conformity](lecture-04.md)
constructs Lagrange elements on simplices and tensor-product cells, their
dual bases and conforming global spaces. An optional extension develops
Raviart–Thomas elements and the Piola transformation.
